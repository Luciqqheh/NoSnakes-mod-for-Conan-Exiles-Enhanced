
NAME: OphidiophobiaMod [Enhanced]

[h2] Update to my original mod RemoveSnakes for Conan Exile Enhanced version [/h2]
[i] --- [/i]

If you don't like Snakes or have any fears/phobias of them, you can now enjoy Conan without worrying about where you go. All snakes has been banished from Exile Lands and Siptah.

This mod removes all snake NPCs
[i] (snake eggs stil lexist but they do nothing) [/i]

If you wish to remove any snake related thing (npc or item) that comes from some mod, just please leave comment with npc name (as example pet_snake_cobra) or item Id and i will update mod - might take few days.
(Only for Enhanced version)

Be aware that there are still some snake realted things in this game - Statues, Colums and some Decorations such as Carpets or Tables with snake motives on them. And also Serpentmen - i havent remove those and i do not plan to since they are not rly snakes, just a bunch of weirdos
